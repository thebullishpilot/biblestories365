About the app

Bible Stories 365 is one retelling a day, in order, from Genesis through Revelation. Each one names the passage it came from and links out to it so you can read the text itself.

This is the adult edition. The accounts are retold as they stand, including the violence, grief, and sexual content they contain. They are original prose, not scripture, and are not a substitute for it.

Common questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases, or tap Restore on the subscription screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account that bought the subscription.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us:

  1. Open the Settings app on your device
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions
  3. Select Bible Stories 365 and tap Cancel Subscription

You can also reach this from Manage Subscription in the app's own Settings. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the period ends stops the next renewal; you keep access until the period you have paid for runs out.

I want a refund.

Refunds are issued by Apple, not by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every story ships inside the app, so the daily reading works with no connection at all. The passage links open Bible Gateway in your browser, and those need a connection.

Will my progress sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Not currently. Reading progress and saved stories are kept on each device separately. Your subscription itself does carry across any device signed in to the same Apple Account.

Something in a story looks wrong.

Please tell me — include the day number and what you spotted. Corrections go into the next update.

The text is too small.

The app follows your system text size. Set it in Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size, or turn on larger accessibility sizes under Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size.
