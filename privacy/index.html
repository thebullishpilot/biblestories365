Privacy Policy

365 Bible Stories · Last updated 7 August 2026

365 Bible Stories does not collect any data about you. There is no account to create, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us, because there is no server to send it to.

What stays on your device

The app stores a small amount of information locally, on your device only. It is never transmitted anywhere, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

WhatWhy
Which days you have readSo the app can show your progress and open on the right day
Your saved storiesSo the favourites list persists between launches
Your display settingsText size, list ordering, and similar preferences
A subscription flagSo the app opens quickly without waiting on the network. Apple remains the source of truth.

Subscriptions

365 Bible Stories offers an auto-renewing yearly subscription. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see and never receive your name, your email address, your payment details, or any part of your Apple Account. The app only ever learns whether an active subscription exists — nothing about who you are.

Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Links to Bible passages

Each story names the passage it is drawn from, and you can tap it to read the text itself. That opens Bible Gateway in your browser. We do not send anything about you along with that link — but once you leave the app, Bible Gateway's own privacy policy applies to what happens there.

Children

This is the adult edition. It retells the biblical accounts as they stand, including violence, grief, and sexual content, and it is not intended for children. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because no data ever leaves your device, there is nothing for us to look up, export, correct, or delete on your behalf. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. If you would like your subscription cancelled, that is done in your Apple Account settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. If a future version of the app ever collects data, this policy and the app's App Store privacy details will both be updated before that version ships.
